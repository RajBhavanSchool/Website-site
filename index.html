01 Our school
A school with a clear compass

Knowledge grows stronger when it grows with character.

At Raj Bhawan, “Good Before Great” is the idea at the heart of our identity.

Our learning vision brings together academic foundations, imagination, movement and responsible use of technology. Children are encouraged to ask thoughtful questions, work with others and grow into confident learners.

02 Our ethos
Growing minds. Grounded hearts.

A beginning that stays with you.

School is where children begin to see what they can do—and who they can become. We imagine an education shaped by curiosity, kindness, clear thinking and the confidence to try again.

“A good education opens the mind. A strong character guides what comes next.”

A word from our Director
From the Director’s desk

Every child deserves a place to become.

Every child arrives with a unique way of seeing the world. Our responsibility is to give that curiosity direction, strengthen it with knowledge and root it in character.

At Raj Bhawan International School, we want children to feel known, encouraged and ready to ask better questions. By bringing strong academic foundations together with creativity, movement and responsible technology, we aim to help every learner grow with confidence—and remember that being good comes before being great.

Director Raj Bhawan International School
